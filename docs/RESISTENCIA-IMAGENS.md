## Prompt comum

Edit the supplied fashion plate for the CLAMA Resistência dress. This is a conservative product-image separation, not a redesign. Preserve the original woman, red bob hair, pose, newspaper corset, chains, black skirt, all EXISTING handwriting placement and text as faithfully as possible, colors and illustration style. Do not add or replace slogans. Full head-to-toe figure and the entire broad skirt hem, with generous white margins on every side. Pure white background. No borders, labels, watermark, or second figure.

## Instrução para frente

Output ONLY the FRONT view (left woman facing the viewer), centered in a portrait image. Remove the right/back woman and her skirt. Preserve the full lateral spread of the front skirt, including its RIGHT edge, which crosses the midpoint of the input. Restore only the tiny obscured outer edge if necessary with continuous black cloth; do not invent new writing.

## Instrução para costas

Output ONLY the BACK view (right woman facing away), centered in a portrait image. Remove the left/front woman and her skirt completely. Preserve the full lateral spread of the back skirt, especially its LEFT edge, which crosses the midpoint of the input. Restore only the tiny obscured outer edge if necessary with continuous black cloth; do not invent new writing. The entire skirt must be intact, not vertically cut off on either side.

# Vistas independentes do vestido Dor

Fonte original: ProjetoSiteModa.Client/public/images/looks/dor-frente-costas.jpg, preservada.

Saídas: ProjetoSiteModa.Client/public/images/looks/dor-frente.png e dor-costas.png.

Foi usada a ferramenta integrada ImageGen para retirar a outra modelo de cada vista e recompor a borda da saia na área sobreposta. São versões editadas para apresentação, não fotografias adicionais da peça. Os arquivos PNG são exibidos por inteiro, sem o recorte lateral que mostrava parte da outra vista.

## Prompts utilizados

### Frente

Edit target: supplied CLAMA fashion plate showing FRONT on left and BACK on right. Deliver ONE isolated FRONT VIEW ONLY, the left woman, whole body and complete dress/hem centered on a plain pure white background, portrait canvas. Preserve exactly the original dress design, grey fabric with black handprints, red sculpted roses, silver teardrops, hair, red veil, face, silhouette and hand position of the left front view. Remove the entire right/back figure and ALL remnants of its skirt. Reconstruct only the small right edge of the front dress skirt that was overlapped. Do not redesign or invent details, do not change color or pose. Match the original fashion illustration/photographic rendering. No text, no second figure, no border, no crop of head or hem. Leave comfortable white space around complete subject.

### Costas

Edit target: supplied CLAMA fashion plate showing FRONT on left and BACK on right. Deliver ONE isolated BACK VIEW ONLY, the right woman facing away, whole body and complete dress/hem centered on a plain pure white background, portrait canvas. Preserve exactly the original rear dress design, grey fabric with black handprints, red sculpted roses, silver teardrops, long brown wavy hair down back, bare upper back, silhouette and hand position of the right view. Remove the entire left/front woman and ALL remnants of the front dress skirt, especially at lower left. Reconstruct only the small left edge of the back skirt where the front skirt overlapped. Do not redesign or invent details, do not change colors or pose. Match original fashion illustration/photographic rendering. No text, no front-facing figure, no second figure, no border, no crop of head or hem. Leave comfortable white space around complete subject.
